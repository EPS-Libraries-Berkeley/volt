## Welcome to STEM Research 101

This module contains two chapters, **Types of Resources** and **Scientific Databases**:

[Types of Resources](https://eps-libraries-berkeley.github.io/volt/resource-types/)
- Learning Outcomes: 
  - Understand that peer-reviewed articles are often, in Western tradition, considered the most credible form of communication in STEM, but other formats are valuable in their openness, speed and breadth of dissemination.
  - Be aware that scientists must communicate information to a wide variety of audiences via different formats or communication styles, and select the format that best fits the intended audience.
  - Articulate the capabilities and constraints of information developed through various creation processes, especially with regard to those specific to STEM information generation.

[Scientific Databases](https://eps-libraries-berkeley.github.io/volt/databases/)
- Learning Outcomes:
  - Assess the scope of an information need and seek appropriate sources in accordance with that need. 
  - Follow the scholarly conversation through time by tracing past and future citations.
